# Introduction

RedCircle is a Solana platform that lets people tokenize and trade on Reddit posts, applying the **Internet Capital Markets** thesis to one of the internet's largest content communities.

Every day a post can go viral and generate enormous attention — yet the value of that attention is captured by no one who created or surfaced it. RedCircle changes that. Posts are tokenized as standard SPL tokens — a tokenized Reddit post is called a **Red post** — so anyone can trade on them, and both the **original creators** and the **curators** who spot great content early earn rewards.

## What you get

- **Attention → assets.** Viral Reddit content becomes a liquid, tradeable token on Solana.
- **Creators get paid.** The original Reddit author earns a share of every trade, claimable in USDC.
- **Curators get rewarded.** Whoever surfaces a post early can register to earn curator rewards.

> Launching a token on RedCircle is free - gasless. And RedCircle never takes custody of your funds or private keys. Trades settle from your own wallet on Solana mainnet.
