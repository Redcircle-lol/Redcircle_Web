# Security & risk

## Non-custodial

RedCircle never takes custody of your funds or private keys. Trades settle from your own wallet.

## Verify before you sign

On-chain transactions are irreversible. Review amounts, price impact, and the destination before confirming.

## Risk disclosure

**Trading is risky.** Token values are highly volatile and can go to zero. Nothing on RedCircle is investment advice. Tokens represent engagement with social posts (Reddit & X) and do not grant ownership, rights, or control over the underlying content.
