# Trading

Every RedCircle token trades against SOL in an on-chain Meteora pool. Trading happens directly from the token page — open a token, review its live price chart, market cap, 24h volume, and holder count, then buy or sell.

## Live pricing

Price, market cap, FDV, 24h volume, and liquidity are sourced from on-chain pool data (via GeckoTerminal) and refreshed continuously.

## Self-custody

Trades settle directly from your connected wallet on Solana mainnet. RedCircle never holds your funds or private keys.

## Finality

Because trades settle on Solana, they are final and irreversible once confirmed. Always review the quoted amount and price impact before confirming a swap.
