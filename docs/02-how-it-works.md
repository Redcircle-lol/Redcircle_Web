# How it works

A RedCircle token moves through four stages, from a viral Reddit or X post to a tradeable market with ongoing rewards.

## 1. Discover

Browse the live feed of tokenized posts from Reddit & X, or paste any Reddit or X post URL. RedCircle continuously syncs trending content so the best candidates surface first.

## 2. Tokenize

Launch the post as an SPL token in a couple of clicks. RedCircle covers the launch cost and signs the transaction server-side, so launching is **free and gasless** for you.

## 3. Trade

Once minted, the token trades against SOL in an on-chain Meteora pool. Anyone can buy and sell; price, market cap, and volume update live.

## 4. Reward

A slice of every trade accrues to the post's creator and curator. They claim their balance in USDC, straight to their Solana wallet.
