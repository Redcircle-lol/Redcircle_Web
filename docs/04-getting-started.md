# Getting started

You can browse, launch, and trade without signing in — you only need a Solana wallet for on-chain actions.

## 1. Connect a Solana wallet

RedCircle supports the standard Solana wallet adapters (Phantom, Solflare, and others). Your wallet is used to trade tokens and to receive curator rewards in USDC. Click **Connect Wallet** in the navbar.

## 2. Sign in with Reddit (creator claims only)

Signing in with Reddit is **not** required to browse, launch tokens, or trade. It is only required when you want to **claim creator rewards** — RedCircle uses your Reddit login to verify you are the original post author before paying out your 0.40% creator fee in USDC.

> You can browse the feed, leaderboard, and any token page without signing in. A wallet is needed to launch and trade. Reddit login is only needed to claim creator rewards.
