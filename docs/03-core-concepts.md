# Core concepts

A handful of ideas explain everything else on the platform.

## Red post

A **Red post** is a tokenized social post (from Reddit or X) — an SPL token (with the `red` suffix) that represents a specific post. Total supply is fixed at **1,000,000,000**. Holding a Red post is a form of investment on that post's continued attention — it does **not** confer ownership of the underlying content.

## Creator

The original author of the tokenized post. They earn **0.40% of every trade**, claimable in USDC. Reddit post authors can sign in with Reddit to verify authorship and claim their creator fee — even if someone else launched the token.

## Curator

The person who surfaces and launches a post. By registering a Solana wallet at launch time, the curator earns **0.15% of every trade** for spotting the content early.

## Pool

The on-chain Meteora liquidity pool where the token trades against SOL. Created automatically at launch through our infrastructure partner, Orynth.
